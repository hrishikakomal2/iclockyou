Pair two phones and get notifications
=====================================
1. Install the app (see below), open it and choose Mingle.
2. Person 1: tap "Create a code" and send the code to your partner. You'll be told when they join.
3. Person 2: tap "I have a code from my partner", type it and tap Connect.
4. Both of you choose what to share. Shared items, status and replies now reach each other from any network.

Turn on notifications (once per phone)
--------------------------------------
- Android (Chrome): open the site, menu > Install app / Add to Home screen. Open the app from the icon, then tap "Turn on notifications".
- iPhone (Safari, iOS 16.4 or newer): open the site in Safari, tap Share > Add to Home Screen, open the app from the new icon (not from Safari), then tap "Turn on notifications".
- Check it: Me > About this test build > Notifications > Test.

What you get
- Updates from your partner arrive as notifications even when the app is closed.
- Alarms, reminders, events and check-in questions arrive as notifications at the right time. They are notifications, not a ringing alarm.

Good to know
- The server relays your shared updates. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so avoid putting sensitive details in shared items.
- Things marked "Only me" never leave your phone.
- The free server may pause after a week with no use. Opening the app wakes it.
- Leaving (Me > Relationship mode > Separate) makes Our Space read-only for both.
