Pairing two phones with a code (Mingle)
======================================
Person 1 (host)
  1. Open I Clock You. During setup choose Mingle (or Me > Relationship mode > Switch to Mingle).
  2. Tap "Create a code". A code like K7Q4-XM9P appears on the Our Space tab.
  3. Tap Share or Copy and send it to your partner.
  4. KEEP THE APP OPEN on that screen until your partner joins.

Person 2 (guest)
  1. Open I Clock You on their own phone. Choose Mingle.
  2. Tap "I have a code from my partner", type the code, tap Connect.
  3. Both phones show "connected" and then let each person choose what to share.

After that
  - Shared items (Our Space, Share with partner), status, check-in updates and replies sync live while both phones are online.
  - My Space stays private on each phone.
  - If one phone is offline, changes wait and sync when both are online again.
  - Reopen the app to reconnect. The first phone that opens waits; the other connects.

Good to know
  - Connection uses a free public relay (PeerJS) for the handshake and then talks phone to phone.
    It can fail on some mobile networks or company Wi-Fi. If it does not connect, try both phones on the same Wi-Fi.
  - The code is one-time: once two phones are paired, other phones are rejected.
  - Leaving (Me > Relationship mode > Separate) makes Our Space read-only for both.
  - Alerts when the app is closed are not reliable in this build.
